    GIS THESIS Data Sharing Policy for Research and Development Students

    Introduction

    This policy outlines the principles and procedures for sharing data between DPS and students engaged in research and development (R&D) activities. Our goal is to facilitate the advancement of knowledge while protecting the integrity of data and respecting the privacy and rights of all stakeholders.

    Policy Statement

    We are committed to promoting a culture of open and responsible data sharing. We recognize that access to data can significantly enhance the quality and efficiency of R&D efforts undertaken by students.

    Data Sharing Principles

  • Transparency : Data shared with R&D students will be accompanied by clear documentation, ensuring that the data can be understood and utilized effectively.
  • Integrity : We will maintain the highest standards of data quality and ensure that data shared is accurate, complete, and reliable.
  • Privacy : Personal and sensitive data will be anonymized before sharing to protect the privacy of individuals. Data will be shared in accordance with applicable laws and regulations.
  • Security : Appropriate security measures will be implemented to prevent unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, or destruction of data.
  • Ethical Use : Data shared with R&D students must be used ethically and responsibly, strictly for the purposes of academic and scientific research.

    • Procedures for Data Sharing

  • Request for Data : Students must submit a formal request outlining the purpose of their research and the specific data required.
  • Review Process : Each request will be reviewed by a designated Data Sharing Committee to ensure compliance with this policy.
  • Data Access Agreement : Approved requests will result in a Data Access Agreement, detailing the terms and conditions of data use.
  • Monitoring and Compliance : Usage of shared data will be monitored, and any breach of the Data Access Agreement will be addressed promptly.

    • Conclusion

    By adhering to this policy, we aim to support the innovative and educational endeavors of R&D students while safeguarding the data and interests of all parties involved.
